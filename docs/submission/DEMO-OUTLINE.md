# Mintmark demo outline

Target length: **3 minutes 30 seconds**. The official maximum is four minutes. Record only after the builder approves the final script.

## Opening — 0:00 to 0:25

Show the landing page and say:

> A ticker is not an onchain identity. NVIDIA has separate token products from Ondo, bStocks, and xStocks, with different contracts and sources. Mintmark lets people start with the company they know and inspect the exact token they found.

## Company-first discovery — 0:25 to 0:55

- Search `NVIDIA`.
- Show the three comparison rows: `NVDAon`, `NVDAB`, and `NVDAx`.
- Point out the separate issuers, exact BSC contracts, and legal-document links.

## Evidence record — 0:55 to 1:35

- Open the Ondo NVIDIA record.
- Show the exact identity key, BscScan link, and evidence classes.
- Expand one issuer-published claim and one onchain-observed claim.
- Show the public record history and state that contract evidence does not prove offchain custody.

## Binance integration — 1:35 to 2:15

- Scroll to the company report.
- Show the exact-contract token market card and its source and coverage notes.
- Show industry, company description, market cap, 52-week range, and 30-day BSC token chart.
- Explain that underlying reference fields and token candles are kept separate.
- Open the xStocks NVIDIA record and show exact-contract market coverage alongside the explicit unavailable underlying-company report.

## Wallet route — 2:15 to 2:50

- Open wallet lookup and use the public example.
- Show that supported positive holdings open their exact registry records.
- State that this read-only lookup checks 24 known Ondo contracts and does not scan every wallet asset.

## Proof and close — 2:50 to 3:30

- Briefly show the public GitHub repository and testing status.
- State: 1,565 checked BSC contracts across Ondo, bStocks, and xStocks; 22 automated tests; public desktop and mobile QA.
- Close with the live URL: `https://mintmark.nuvixes.studio`.
