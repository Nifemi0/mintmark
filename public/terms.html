Using the data

Free, including commercially, with credit.

Not advice

Information only.

Mintmark identifies token contracts and shows where each claim comes from. It is not investment, legal or tax advice, does not recommend buying or selling any token, and does not place trades. Trading links lead to third-party venues whose availability depends on your region.

Accuracy

Provided as is.

Mintmark checks contracts onchain and compares them with what providers publish, but data can be incomplete, delayed or wrong, and providers can change their lists at any time. Mintmark does not verify that any token is backed by real shares. The data is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind, and Mintmark is not liable for losses from relying on it. For decisions involving money, confirm the contract with the issuer.

API use

Fair use, stable shapes.

  • The API is limited to 120 requests a minute per client; use the bulk lookup for many addresses.
  • Do not try to get around limits, overload the service, or scrape it in a way that degrades it for others.
  • Within v1, response fields may be added but are not renamed or removed. Breaking changes go to a new version, announced in the daily report first.
  • Access can be limited for clients that break these terms.
