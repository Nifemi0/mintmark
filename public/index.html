How to read Mintmark

Different claims. Different proof.

Observed onchain

The BSC contract exists and reports its token name and symbol. Open the explorer to inspect it.

Published by issuer

The issuer lists the contract, underlying company, or terms. The source link shows the publisher.

Still unverified

Mintmark does not infer share custody, legal rights, or token equivalence from a ticker or contract alone.
