Mintmark documentation

Identity before inference.

Mintmark is a public identity and evidence registry for tokenized stocks on BNB Smart Chain. It helps someone begin with a familiar company, find every checked provider product, and inspect the exact contract and source behind each claim.

Quick start

Find the product you actually mean.

  1. Search a company or ticker.Use NVIDIA, Tesla, Apple, NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, a fund, a keyword, or a complete BSC contract.
  2. Compare provider products.A shared stock ticker can resolve to separate Ondo, bStocks, and xStocks contracts. Each row remains a distinct product.
  3. Open the evidence record.Check the full contract, BscScan, issuer terms, onchain name and symbol, source differences, and public history.
  4. Read live data in context.Exact-contract token-market coverage stays separate from the underlying-company report. Missing fields and provider inconsistencies remain visible.

Shareable discovery

Link directly to a comparison or record.

Put a familiar public ticker in the q parameter. Opening a record adds its exact 56:contract key while preserving the comparison context.

The search parameter is for discovery. Mintmark still uses chain ID plus the complete contract address as product identity.

Public API

Small endpoints with explicit boundaries.

All endpoints are read-only. Live Binance-backed routes depend on server-side credentials; no API secret is sent to the browser.

RouteReturns
GET /api/healthService status, catalog count, snapshot time, and Binance configuration state.
GET /api/records?q=NVDASearch results and compact comparison evidence across all checked providers.
GET /api/record?key=56:0x…One exact registry record, its identity fields, documents, and evidence.
GET /api/report?key=56:0x…Exact-contract token-market coverage plus an underlying-company report when supported.
GET /api/history?key=56:0x…Public record versions and unresolved source differences.
GET /api/wallet?address=0x…Read-only lookup over the 24 explicitly supported Ondo contracts.

Evidence model

Every label says what was actually checked.

Onchain observed

Mintmark read contract bytecode, name, or symbol from BSC RPC at the recorded time.

Issuer published

The provider published the identity, contract, or terms. Mintmark links that statement.

Third-party reported

Binance supplied the field. Provider and retrieval times remain attached to live market data.

Unverified

Mintmark has not independently inspected offchain backing, custody, or legal rights.

A working ERC-20 contract proves its onchain responses. It does not prove that shares are held, that products with the same ticker are equivalent, or that reported liquidity is executable.

Limits

What Mintmark does not claim.

  • The catalog is a dated, reviewed snapshot and can become stale.
  • Market fields are provider reports, not official exchange quotes or liquidity guarantees.
  • The wallet route checks 24 known Ondo contracts and does not scan every wallet asset.
  • Mintmark does not execute transactions, recommend investments, or verify custody.
  • A missing provider row is a data gap, not proof that a token is invalid or retired.
